Shigeki Miyazaki

Institute of Science Tokyo, Department of Physics, Particle Physics Group
My research interests lie in supersymmetric and conformal field theories, particularly four-dimensional \(\mathcal{N} = 2\) superconformal field theories. Currently, I am focusing on topics such as the 4d/2d (SCFT/VOA) correspondence, Argyres–Douglas theories, \(\mathcal{W}\)-algebras, and the ODE/IM correspondence.
